Executive Boxes

Treat your guests to an intimate day out in our executive boxes crafting your own private matchday experience.

Whether it's staff, clients, or loved ones - enjoy the game in your very own domain at Ewood Park.

Take a look around an Executive Box

  • Space to host up to 10 or 20 guests (minimum of six guests required)

  • Choice of a pre-match one-course hot buffet with plated dessert or four-course meal

  • Pay bar facility

  • Matchday gift for matchday bookings

  • Crowd-facing advertising board for seasonal bookings

  • Reserved parking space (one space per four people)

Seasonal Ticket benefits:

  • Company or family name listed in the Digital Matchday Supplement

  • An invite to four club events per season

  • Priority access to away match tickets

  • A premium gift (one per party)

  • A crowd-facing advertising board in the Blackburn End
