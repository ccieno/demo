## Maldives

Flight time 10hr 30 direct to Malé · GMT +5 · Best months November to April · Free visa on arrival · Typical trip 7–10 nights

### Kuramathi Island Resort — Rasdhoo Atoll, from £1,949 per person for 7 nights

**How do guests reach Kuramathi from Malé airport?**
A 90-minute speedboat transfer departs daily and is included in the holiday price.

**Does Kuramathi have a house reef?**
Yes, the house reef runs the full length of the island with three marked entry points straight off the beach.

**Is Kuramathi suitable for children?**
Yes, Kuramathi welcomes all ages and runs a supervised kids' club for children aged 3 to 12.

**What board basis does Kuramathi include?**
All-inclusive is the standard board basis, covering all meals, snacks and drinks across the island's restaurants and bars.

**How large is the island?**
Kuramathi is 1.8 kilometres long and takes about 20 minutes to walk end to end along the sand.

### Baros Maldives — North Malé Atoll, from £2,640 per person for 7 nights

**How long is the transfer to Baros?**
A 25-minute speedboat runs around the clock, so late-arriving flights still reach the island the same day.

**Is Baros adults only?**
Baros accepts guests aged 12 and over, which keeps the island quiet and adult in feel.

**What is the snorkelling like at Baros?**
A continuous coral reef encircles the island roughly 200 metres offshore and is reachable by swimming from any beach.

**What board basis does Baros include?**
Bed and breakfast is standard, with half board and full board available as upgrades at the time of booking.

**How many villas does Baros have?**
Baros has 75 villas split between overwater and beachfront, and every one has its own private deck.

### Six Senses Laamu — Laamu Atoll, from £3,880 per person for 7 nights

**How do guests reach Six Senses Laamu?**
A 55-minute domestic flight from Malé to Kadhdhoo is followed by a 20-minute speedboat to the resort.

**What makes Laamu Atoll different from the rest of the Maldives?**
Six Senses Laamu is the only resort in the entire atoll, so its reefs and surf breaks are effectively private.

**Is Six Senses Laamu suitable for families?**
Yes, the resort runs a kids' club called The Den for children aged 4 to 12 alongside adult wellness programming.

**Does Six Senses Laamu have overwater villas?**
Yes, 65 of its villas sit on stilts over the lagoon with direct water access and overwater hammocks.

**What sustainability measures does the resort operate?**
The resort is single-use-plastic free, bottles its own drinking water on site, and grows much of its produce in an organic garden.

### Soneva Fushi — Baa Atoll, from £4,495 per person for 7 nights

**How do guests reach Soneva Fushi?**
A 30-minute seaplane from Malé lands directly at the resort, operating during daylight hours only.

**What is the barefoot policy at Soneva Fushi?**
Guests remove their shoes on arrival and keep them off for the whole stay, which is a signature of the resort.

**When can guests swim with manta rays?**
Hanifaru Bay in Baa Atoll draws large manta aggregations between May and November, and the resort runs boats there in season.

**What do the villas at Soneva Fushi include?**
Every villa has a private pool, and several also have their own waterslide running from the upper deck.

**What entertainment is on the island?**
Soneva Fushi has an open-air overwater cinema and an astronomical observatory with a resident astronomer.

## Mexico

Flight time 10hr 15 direct to Cancún · GMT −5 · Best months December to April · No visa required for UK passport holders · Typical trip 7–14 nights

### Iberostar Selection Paraíso — Riviera Maya, from £1,299 per person for 7 nights

**What does the all-inclusive cover at Iberostar Selection Paraíso?**
All meals, snacks, local and international drinks and every à la carte restaurant are included with no supplements.

**How far is the resort from Cancún airport?**
The transfer takes about 45 minutes by road.

**Is Iberostar Selection Paraíso good for families?**
Yes, it has a kids' club for ages 4 to 12 and an on-site water park with slides.

**What is the beach like?**
The resort fronts roughly one kilometre of private white-sand beach with direct sea access.

**Is there golf at the resort?**
Yes, an 18-hole championship course sits within the complex and resort guests receive priority tee times.

### Grand Velas Riviera Maya — Playa del Carmen, from £2,180 per person for 7 nights

**What does the all-inclusive cover at Grand Velas?**
Premium branded spirits, all eight restaurants including the fine-dining venues, and in-suite minibar are all included.

**How is the resort laid out?**
Grand Velas is divided into three areas: Zen Grand set in jungle, Ambassador on the beachfront, and adults-only Grand Class for guests 18 and over.

**What are the rooms like?**
Every unit is a suite of at least 100 square metres, and many have a private plunge pool on the terrace.

**What is included at the spa?**
The 8,900-square-metre spa includes its hydrotherapy water circuit at no charge for guests booking any treatment.

**How far is Grand Velas from Cancún airport?**
The drive takes about 40 minutes.

### Rosewood Mayakoba — Playa del Carmen, from £2,950 per person for 7 nights

**What board basis does Rosewood Mayakoba include?**
Bed and breakfast is the standard board basis.

**How do guests get to their suite?**
Guests travel by private boat along the resort's freshwater lagoon canals to reach their suite.

**Do the suites have private pools?**
Yes, every suite at Rosewood Mayakoba has its own private plunge pool.

**Is there golf at Mayakoba?**
Yes, guests have access to El Camaleón, the Greg Norman course that has hosted the PGA Tour.

**Where is the spa?**
The spa occupies its own private island within the resort's lagoon, reached by boat or footbridge.

### Nizuc Resort & Spa — Cancún, from £2,395 per person for 7 nights

**What board basis does Nizuc include?**
Half board is standard, covering breakfast and dinner each day.

**Where exactly is Nizuc?**
Nizuc sits on the Punta Nizuc headland at the southern tip of the Cancún hotel zone, about 10 minutes from the airport.

**How many beaches and pools does the resort have?**
Nizuc has two private beaches and six swimming pools across the property.

**Are there adults-only areas?**
Yes, one pool and a dedicated section of beach are reserved for adults.

**What dining is available?**
Six restaurants operate on site, spanning Mexican, Peruvian, Asian and Mediterranean menus.

## Bali

Flight time 17hr with one stop · GMT +8 · Best months April to October · Visa on arrival around £25 · Typical trip 10–14 nights

### Padma Resort Ubud — Ubud, from £1,575 per person for 10 nights

**What board basis does Padma Resort Ubud include?**
Bed and breakfast is standard, with a full buffet breakfast served daily.

**What is the pool like?**
The resort has an 89-metre infinity pool overlooking the valley, the longest in the Ubud area.

**How far is the resort from central Ubud?**
Padma sits in Payangan about 25 minutes north of central Ubud, and a complimentary shuttle runs to town daily.

**Is Padma Resort Ubud suitable for families?**
Yes, it offers connecting family rooms and a supervised kids' club.

**Which activities are included in the rate?**
Daily yoga, guided jungle treks and Balinese cooking classes are all included at no extra cost.

### The Legian Seminyak — Seminyak, from £2,240 per person for 10 nights

**What board basis does The Legian Seminyak include?**
Bed and breakfast is the standard board basis.

**What are the rooms like?**
Every unit at The Legian is a suite, and all of them face the Indian Ocean.

**How close is the hotel to the beach?**
The Legian sits directly on Seminyak Beach with private access straight from the grounds.

**Is there an adults-only option?**
Yes, The Club at The Legian is a separate villa compound reserved for guests aged 16 and over.

**How far are the Seminyak shops and beach clubs?**
The main Seminyak boutiques and beach clubs are a 10-minute walk from the hotel.

### Four Seasons Sayan — Ubud, from £3,410 per person for 10 nights

**What is the arrival at Four Seasons Sayan like?**
Guests cross a 60-metre teak suspension bridge into a rooftop lotus pond before descending into the resort.

**What board basis does Four Seasons Sayan include?**
Bed and breakfast is the standard board basis.

**Where is the resort?**
It sits in the Ayung River valley about 15 minutes from central Ubud.

**How many rooms does it have?**
Four Seasons Sayan has 60 suites and villas, and most of the villas have a private pool.

**What wellness experiences are included?**
The resort's Sacred Nap sound-healing session and its river water-purification ceremony are both included for staying guests.

### Six Senses Uluwatu — Uluwatu, from £3,690 per person for 10 nights

**What board basis does Six Senses Uluwatu include?**
Bed and breakfast is the standard board basis.

**What is the setting?**
The resort stands on a clifftop roughly 100 metres above the Indian Ocean with uninterrupted west-facing views.

**Does the resort have its own beach?**
Six Senses Uluwatu has no beach of its own; a funicular takes guests down to the Cloud Nine pool and rock pool, and Padang Padang beach is a 10-minute drive away.

**Is it good for surfing?**
The resort overlooks the Uluwatu reef break, one of the most consistent waves in Bali, which is best from April to October.

**Do the villas have private pools?**
Yes, every villa at Six Senses Uluwatu has its own private pool.
